The rules & your privacy

Short version, in plain English.

Who can use Turnout

You need to be 16 or older. Turnout is for finding real parties with real people. Don’t use it if you’re younger.

What we store

Your name, ZIP, email, and profile picture. If you host, the party’s address. When you check in, the fact that you were there and roughly when. Photos you put in an album or send in a message.

Who can see it

A party address is only readable by the host and people holding a ticket — it is locked in the database, not just hidden in the app. Album photos are only visible to people who checked in at that party. Messages and the photos in them are only visible to you and the person you sent them to. Your home address, if you save one, is never shown to anyone.

Location

Check-in compares your location to the host’s spot on your phone and sends the result, not a trail of where you’ve been. Party pins on the map are deliberately fuzzed until you’re in.

What’s not allowed

Fake parties, harassment, posting someone’s address without permission, photos of people who don’t want to be posted, and anything illegal. Report anyone doing it — every profile and party has a report option, and blocking someone cuts contact both ways.

Parties are not ours

Turnout helps you find a party. It doesn’t run it, vouch for it, or take responsibility for what happens there. Use your judgement, tell someone where you’re going, and look out for each other.

Deleting your stuff

Profile → Delete my account removes your account, your parties, your photos and your messages for good.

Questions

Message Cade on the app or at jointurnout.app.
